Duograin Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Duograin is a half-frame film camera app for iPhone. It is built so that your photos stay yours: it works entirely on your device, without an account, ads, analytics or tracking.

Data we collect

None. Duograin does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. There is no account and no sign-in. The app contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising and no analytics.

Camera

Duograin uses the camera only while you are on the camera screen, to show the viewfinder and take the photos and short clips you shoot. Clips are silent: Duograin never uses the microphone. Images from the camera are processed on your iPhone and are never uploaded.

Your photos

Photos and clips you shoot are saved inside the app on your iPhone (and in your device backups, if you use them). When a roll is developed, Duograin can copy the developed photos and clips to your Photos library; for this it asks for add-only access, so it can add photos but cannot read your library. You can turn automatic saving off in Settings. To develop photos you already have, you choose them in Apple’s photo picker: Duograin receives only the photos you pick, never the rest of your library. Photos only leave your phone when you share them yourself (for example with the share sheet or a shared roll).

Shared rolls

A shared roll is shot on two iPhones. You and your friend swap roll files (.duoroll) yourselves, with AirDrop, Messages, Mail or Files. A roll file contains the roll’s name and film, the name you enter for yourself (shown to your friend beside your photos), the original photos and clips you shot on that roll with their settings, and, if you sealed the roll, its opening day and your note. It goes only to the people you send it to: Duograin has no server and never sees it. When you open a friend’s file, the app checks it and keeps its photos inside the app on your iPhone, like your own. Anyone you send a file to can keep it and the photos in it.

Sealed rolls and widgets

When you seal a roll, its opening day and the note you write are kept inside the app on your iPhone. Duograin’s widgets read a small copy that the app keeps in a shared folder on your iPhone (an App Group): the countdowns of your sealed rolls, and small copies of a few photos from rolls you have already opened. Photos from a sealed roll, or a roll you have not opened yet, are never copied there. Widgets show this only on your own Home Screen and Lock Screen; nothing is sent anywhere.

Notifications

If you choose a later development time or seal a roll, Duograin can schedule a local notification on your iPhone to tell you when the roll is ready or its day has come. Notifications are created on the device; no data is sent anywhere.

Purchases

Film packs are one-time In-App Purchases handled by Apple through the App Store. Duograin never sees your payment details. The app only learns which packs your Apple Account owns so it can unlock them, and it keeps that list on your device.

Children

Duograin does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete a roll inside the app (it stays in Recently Deleted for 30 days, then is removed), or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Photos you saved to your Photos library are managed by the Photos app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
