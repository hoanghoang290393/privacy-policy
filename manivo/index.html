Manivo Privacy Policy

Manivo: Nail Before & After is an iOS app by an independent developer that turns two photos into a before & after video. Everything happens on your iPhone. The app collects no personal data.

1Summary

Manivo has no account, no sign-in, no server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your photos, videos, logo and notes stay on your device. The developer never receives any of it.

2Sharing your videos

When you tap Save & Share, the video is saved to Photos and the iOS share sheet opens. Anything you then post or send goes to the app or person you choose, under their privacy policy. Manivo can also copy a caption to your clipboard for you to paste; it does not read the clipboard.

3Purchases (Apple App Store)

Manivo Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly) or a one-time lifetime purchase through Apple's App Store. Apple handles your payment details and receipt; the developer only learns from StoreKit that Pro is active on your device. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

4Children's privacy

Manivo is a general-audience tool for nail artists and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

5Your choices & rights

  • Delete a project: tap the trash button on its thumbnail on the Home screen.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the app; its storage is removed with it. Videos you saved to Photos stay in your library until you delete them there.
  • Permissions: turn camera, Photos or notifications on or off at any time in iPhone Settings → Manivo.

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to export or erase on our side, wherever you live (EU/UK GDPR, California CCPA and similar laws).

6Changes to this policy

If Manivo ever starts using a new service that handles data, this page will be updated before that version is released and the date above will change.
